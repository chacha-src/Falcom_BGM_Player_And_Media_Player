Bonus: the Soft3D aerial race

An aerial race opened from the Race button on the media player's bottom row. You follow a ribbon of light — the power band — and compete for position against AI craft. Like the maze, it exists as a diversion while music plays.

Soft3D sky race

Controls

ControlAction
Mouse movementSteer the craft (yaw and pitch)
Left buttonAccelerate
Right buttonBrake
WheelZoom
Middle buttonLook back

Gamepads are supported: left stick steers, RT or A accelerates, LT or B brakes, the hat provides alternative steering and the right stick offsets the camera.

To invert up and down for the mouse and pad, choose Invert Y in the Y combo. The setting is saved.

Reading the screen

The power band (ribbon of light)
The course itself. Flying on the band regenerates thrust, and the inner green strip down the centre restores HP.
Start / lap line
The chequered gate is the lap marker. Crossing it forwards counts a lap, and the last lap is the finish.
Off the band
You can fly freely, which makes shortcuts possible — but when the thrust gauge hits zero you cannot move and are returned to the point where you left the band. Your HP is untouched.
Terrain (hills, valleys, rivers)
Real relief. Trees and other props grow from hill surfaces. The band may clip into terrain and trees. Large mountains stay solid from the outside; where the band meets them a tunnel is carved through. Geometry between the camera and the course fades so you can still see the lane. Fly the gaps. Contact costs HP.
Trees, buildings and hazards
Trunks and buildings are allowed to sink into the band. You fly between branches, pipes and structures. Nothing is placed so that it fills the whole lane. A hit costs about one tenth of your HP and knocks you toward the open side.
Craft contact
Bumping another craft knocks both of you slightly and costs about one thirtieth of HP.
Rival HP and retirement
Rivals use the same HP, obstacle hits and knockback. At 0 HP they explode and retire on the spot; others overtake so their rank keeps falling. Super easy caps retirements at about four of twelve crafts. Intense can still see retirements, but is tuned so you can win. AI level shows as weaving, attempted shortcuts, braking and leaving the lane.
Item orbs
Touching one changes the playing track's tempo or pitch, and applies a race effect such as a boost or fog at the same time. A small label sits above each orb so you can tell the type.
The standings panel
Below the minimap on the right: name, position and recent lap times. Your own row is tinted differently.

From start to finish

  1. Press Generate and a course is built, followed straight away by a demo lap. It shows you the whole course from above, which makes a useful reconnaissance run. The mouse pans the view. Each generate also picks a plan: an oval, a figure-8 whose crossing is split in height so you cannot cut through it on the flat, a rounded convex shape, or a concave pinch.
  2. Press Start to end the demo. After a five-count and GO, the race begins.
  3. Complete your chosen number of laps and the podium shows the top three.

What you can set

  • AI — super easy, easy, normal, hard or intense (super easy stays a bit slower and weaves more; intense holds a tighter line but is still beatable)
  • Opponents — how many rivals
  • Length — auto, short or long
  • Laps — how many times round
  • Theme — forest, ruins, oil factory, night city, underwater tube, grassland, sunset mesa or cloud garden
  • Density — Light (the previous coarseness), 1–9, Dense, 11–19, or Fine. Smoothes terrain, mountains and trees. Steps of ×8; Dense is about 80×, Fine about 160×. Geometry past one vertex-buffer cap is split across draws. Default is 5 (about 40×). Shared with the maze and saved.

Fifty license-free photo textures (512px) are embedded in the exe: theme albedo plus detail, water, craft, cloud cards and a sky cube. Wrap lighting and reflection; items are crystal orbs with a small label above each so you can tell the type. No extra files are placed in the same location as the executable.

These settings persist, so the next time you open the race you start on the same terms.

PCM sound effects

Engine notes are synthesized as PCM, not taken from wave files, together with the start countdown and GO, lap crossings, finish, podium, course-out, item pickups, collisions and wrong-way alerts. Throttle raises pitch and sub-bass, each craft has its own timbre, and distance changes loudness and left/right pan (in the spirit of DirectSound 3D). They are not mixed into the music streaming buffer. Toggle them from View in the right-click menu.

The right-click menu

Right-click the status area or the window chrome for restart, regenerate course, minimap display and item types. On the view itself the right button is the brake, so use the status area when you want the menu.

Tip

The camera deliberately lags behind on both axes to reduce motion sickness. On steep climbs it eases to a look-up from below. Terrain varies by theme with hills, valleys and rivers. The band may clip through trees and ground; large mountains are tunneled through.

Please note

This cannot be open at the same time as Bonus: the Soft3D maze, and it will not start on a system without DirectX 11.
