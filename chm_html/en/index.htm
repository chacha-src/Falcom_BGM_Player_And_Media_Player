Raira Help

Welcome to the help for the media player "Raira" (oggYSEDbgm). This program is two things in one: a player that loops Nihon Falcom game music exactly the way it loops in game, and a full-featured media player for your everyday music and video.

New here?

Start with Your first launch. It explains the "which screen would you like to start in?" question you get on first run, and the handful of settings worth doing straight away.

If you came for game music, jump to Pointing Raira at your game folders. If you came to play your own music collection, A tour of the media player screen is the fastest route.

How to read this help

  • Every page has Back and Next links at the bottom. Reading top to bottom takes you from the simple things to the deeper ones.
  • The "See also" box at the end of each page links to related features. That is the quickest way to discover that one feature quietly connects to another.
  • The 日本語 link at the top of every page opens the same page in Japanese.
  • The Index tab on the left accepts both English and Japanese words.
Tip

Lost track of where a feature lives? Press the "?" button on any window. You get a diagram made for that screen. See The "?" guide on every window.
