CEmu: FM-7

FM-7 and FM77AV zips that have been confirmed to play. They live in data\fm7. FM TOWNS packs whose catalog row is pc98vx play on PC-98 with the 80286 and OPNA.

CPU

The CPU is the 6809. FM-7 and FM77AV share it. Only the sound chip changes.

Sound chips

ColumnPartWhat it is
PSGAY-3-8910The FM-7 PSG.
OPNYM2203The FM77AV FM chip.

When the same zip name is confirmed for both FM-7 and FM77AV, both PSG and OPN are marked. Anything not listed still will not play.
