Looking back at play history

The play history timeline lists what you have listened to, newest first, under date headers. It is the place to go for "what was that track playing last week?"

Reading it

  • Tracks appear under a header for the day you played them.
  • Up to 64 entries are kept.
  • Select a row to play that track again or add it to a playlist.

When it comes in handy

You skipped past something good
When a track you were enjoying gets replaced by the next one, the history gets you straight back.
You want to keep the sequence
Found a run of tracks that worked? Move them from the history into a playlist to keep it. See Working with playlists.
As a practice record
Read alongside the weekly summary and practice log in The companion tools to see how much you have actually put in.

Publishing what is playing now

Separately from the history, Raira can write the current track name to a text file. A nowplaying.txt appears next to the executable, ready for streaming software to display on screen. See The companion tools and Live streaming.

Tip

Frequently played tracks also show up in the taskbar jump list when you right-click the icon, so you can start one without opening the program. See Shortcuts and the command palette.
