Bonus: the Soft3D maze

A first-person DirectX 11 maze, opened from the Maze button on the media player's bottom row. It is there as a light distraction while music plays, quite separate from the playback features. Your progress autosaves, so reopening it puts you back where you were.

Reading the map

  • A minimap sits in the top right.
  • SPACE or middle-click switches to the full overview. Esc closes it too.
  • While the overview is open, the wheel zooms and dragging scrolls.
  • Shift+wheel, or Left and Right, change floor.
  • Unvisited areas are black; visited areas are light blue.

Going underground

The basement combo adds nought to three underground floors.

  • Wall and floor motifs change per floor: brick and greenery on the ground floor, damp stone on B1, rusted metal on B2 and volcanic rock on B3. They are license-free photo textures (512px, 50 in the exe) — albedo plus detail per floor, item/gimmick/glass maps; no extra files. Basements use an indoor cube and brighter lighting so walls stay readable, with reflection and wrap lighting.
  • Orange stairs go down and cyan stairs go up, moving you two squares diagonally.
  • There are translucent traps — slime, spikes, ice and darkness — plus windows and mirrors; they carry a small label above them. None of them warp you.
  • Old saves where stairs landed on the same square are regenerated automatically.
  • The goal sits on whichever floor the difficulty decides.

Difficulty

From very easy to very hard. Harder settings mean narrower corridors, more stairs and more back-and-forth between floors, and traps become more common.

Mesh density

The Density combo sets how finely walls, floors and orbs are meshed. Light is the previous coarseness, 1–9 and 11–19 step up by ×8 each, Dense is about 80×, and Fine is the maximum (about 160×). Geometry past one vertex-buffer cap is split across draws. The default is 5 (about 40×). It is shared with the race and saved.

Items that change the music

Touching a floating orb affects the track that is playing: tempo up or down, pitch up or down, previous or next track, volume, EQ and flatten, reverb, crossfade and random play. They are placed on every floor. See The bonus games for the full list.

The windows in the walls are decorative and block movement.

Sound effects

Items, keys, doors, stairs and portals are made on the spot on the fly, along with wall bumps, locked doors, spike/slime/ice/dark traps and reaching the goal. They use a path separate from the playing track, so the music is left alone. You can turn them off from View in the right-click menu.

Tip

Pushed tempo and pitch too far? The right-click menu has "reset tempo/pitch to open values", which restores them to what they were when you opened the maze.

Please note

This cannot be open at the same time as Bonus: the Soft3D aerial race. If you see "DirectX 11 initialization failed", try updating your graphics driver.
