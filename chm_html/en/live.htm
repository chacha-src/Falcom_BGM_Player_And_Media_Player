Live streaming

Tick Live in Recording your screen and Raira sends picture and sound to your platform instead of writing an MP4. YouTube Live, Niconico and custom RTMP destinations are supported.

What you need first

Please note

ffmpeg.exe must sit next to the executable, or in the ogg_binary folder. Without it, streaming will not start.

Streaming to YouTube Live

  1. Enter your Google OAuth Client ID and Secret.
  2. Run the authentication to connect your account.
  3. Create the broadcast, choosing a title and privacy (public, unlisted or private).
  4. Start streaming.

For a first attempt, set the privacy to private. You can check your framing and levels and switch to public afterwards.

Streaming to Niconico or a custom RTMP URL

  1. Open your platform's broadcast page and find the RTMP URL and stream key.
  2. Enter both into this window.
  3. Start streaming.

Audio quality

Choose an AAC profile.

  • 128 kbps for ordinary streaming.
  • 192 kbps when the music itself matters.
  • Low-latency 96 kbps when you want to keep the delay down.

Making the stream look good

  • The window composition in Recording your screen puts a game and the media player side by side.
  • The /overlay page in Controlling Raira from a phone or PC is simple HTML with a transparent background showing the track and state — drop it into an OBS browser source.
  • The current track can also be written to nowplaying.txt. See The companion tools.
  • Continuous-play crossfade keeps track changes smooth.
Tip

You cannot leave the desk mid-stream, so keep Controlling Raira from a phone or PC open — you can change tracks and volume from your phone. There is even a shared queue if you want your audience choosing what comes next.

Please note

While Live is on, no MP4 file is written. If you want a copy afterwards, record separately or rely on your platform's archive.
