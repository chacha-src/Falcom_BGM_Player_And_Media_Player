Changing tempo and pitch

Tempo (speed) and pitch (how high the notes sound) are separate controls. Slow a track down while leaving the pitch alone, or raise the pitch without touching the speed.

Changing the tempo

Only the speed changes. Pitch stays where it is, so voices and instruments keep their proper notes even when you slow things right down.

  • One-press presets at 50%, 75% and 100%.
  • Continuous adjustment across 50–200%.
  • For working phrases out by ear, combine with Cues, A-B and practice tempo.

Changing the pitch

Only the pitch changes; the speed is untouched.

  • Transpose a song to suit your own vocal range.
  • Match an instrument's tuning — pairs well with Tuner practice.
  • To confirm the key of a track, the key-detect tool in The companion tools stores the analysed key on the track.

Moving these over time

For something like "speed up gradually from the thirty-second mark", use Automating a performance with prompts. Write it as text, or place it on a timeline with Laying commands out on the roll.

Moving them by hand

Playing with the DJ pad puts tempo and pitch under buttons alongside beat nudges and scratching, so you can work them like an instrument. With Controlling Raira from a MIDI keyboard you can assign them to real keys and knobs.

Tip

The bonus Soft3D maze and Soft3D aerial race have floating orbs that change tempo and pitch when you touch them — the same controls, reached from the other direction.

Please note

Large tempo or pitch shifts can make the sound coarse. When exporting, "Apply prompt execution" in Audio export (WAV / mp3 / FLAC) gives the cleanest result.
