Jacket art and the banner display

The upper part of the screen shows the "face" of the track. A ring runs around the jacket art to show remaining time, and beside it the level and left-right balance move with the music.

Jacket art

Raira shows artwork embedded in the track's tags. If there is none, it looks for cover.jpg in the same folder as the track.

  • Right-click to load a different jacket or save the current image to a file.
  • Select tracks in the playlist and right-click to refresh jackets for the whole selection.
  • To replace the artwork inside the file itself, use Editing tags.

The remaining-time ring

A thin ring travels around the jacket to show how much of the track is left, so you can tell at a glance without reading numbers.

Banner modes

Beside the jacket, a banner moves with the audio. Pick its style from the right-click menu.

Bars
Bars per frequency band rise and fall — the familiar one.
Mirror
The display spreads out symmetrically.
Wave
The waveform itself scrolls past.

The phase correlation meter (phi / LR)

The small meter to the right of the banner shows how similar the left and right channels are.

  • Swinging to the right means the channels agree closely — close to mono.
  • Swinging to the left means they are moving against each other. At the extreme, the sound can cancel out on mono playback.
  • If that worries you, Tools for shaping the sound has one-click M/S presets for narrow, wide and mono.
Tip

If a title or artist is not what you expected, fix the display name in Viewing and fixing file info. To rewrite the file's own tags, use Editing tags.

Please note

Turning on the acrylic blur makes this area translucent too. See Acrylic look and context menus.
