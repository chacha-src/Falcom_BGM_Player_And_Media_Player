SASAMI .mpsmv — delta from .mpw2

The MIDI body is .mpw2 (same opcodes as .mpy). A trailing MPW3 block loads HALion-style VSTs per part on the host, not KPI fmmidi. Same idea as playing .mid through VST.

MPW3 trailer

Right after the MIDI body: magic MPW3, u32 version (1 or 2), then 32 parts:

FieldSizeSource
path520 bytes@VST"…" / staff Tone
prog / msb / lsb / forceCh4 each@PROG @BANK
comp bloblen+bytes@VSTSTATEB64 / + continuation
ctrl bloblen+bytes@VSTCTRLB64

Version ≥ 2 may append MACR (name 64 wchar + body) for @MACRO / @CALL.

Insert FX

@VSTFX slot "path" and @VSTFXSTATEB64 — staff Insert FX.

When Composer writes .mpsmv

  • Any @VST / state / FX bind.
  • @METER, @MACRO, @CC family, native ｛:.
  • Staff Tone row has a VST path.

Playback opens the trailer VSTs and routes notes to those parts — not GM mapper piano. Long @VSTSTATEB64 lines can be folded in the text UI.
