CEmu: PC-88

A lookup for PC-8800 zip packs. How to listen is in Bonus: old-machine sound. The PC-98 table is CEmu: PC-98.

CPU

The PC-8801 CPU is the Z80. Every row here uses that one CPU.

The other CPU is the V30, used when the catalog says PC-88VA or VA DOS. OPN / OPNA below still describe the sound.

Sound chips

ColumnPartWhat it is
OPNYM22038801 FM. Common on packs from before Sound Board II.
OPNAYM2608Sound Board II packs.

The table lists only zips confirmed to play. A catalog entry that is not here still will not play. A circle is a confirmed route. PC-88VA rows are marked VA (V30) in Notes. 8801 rows stay on the Z80.
