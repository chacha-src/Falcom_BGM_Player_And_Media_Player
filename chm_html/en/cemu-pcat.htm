CEmu: PC/AT

DOS PC/AT zips that have been confirmed to play. They live in data\pc.

CPU

The CPU is the 80286. Same number as the PC-98, different sound path.

Sound chips

ColumnPartWhat it is
OPL2YM3812AdLib and Sound Blaster. Confirmed SB16 packs use this OPL2, not a YMF262.
CMSSAA1099Game Blaster.
beepPC beepPacks whose Notes say beep.

GS / SC-88 / MT-32 MIDI and OPL3 exist as routes, but no confirmed PC/AT pack uses them yet, so they are not columns. A zip that is not listed still will not play.
