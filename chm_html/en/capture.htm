Recording your screen

Open this from the Capture button on the media player's bottom row. It records your screen as MP4. Beyond capturing a whole monitor, you can lay several windows out as layers and record them as one picture.

Three modes

Primary monitor
Records your main monitor as it is. The simplest option.
All monitors (virtual desktop)
Records every connected monitor as one large image.
Window composition
Places the windows you choose as layers — the mode for building a stream layout.

Building a composition

  1. Switch to composition mode and add the windows you want to show.
  2. Drag them around on the preview to position them.
  3. Grab a corner handle to resize.
  4. Change the Z-order to decide what sits in front.

Tick Include MP song and the open media player window itself joins the composition — handy when you want the track title and artwork on screen.

Audio settings

  • System audio brings in whatever your PC is playing (loopback).
  • Microphone brings in your voice. Turn on Apply VC and it arrives with the same preset as The voice changer.

Picture settings

  • FPS — frames per second. Higher is smoother and produces bigger files.
  • Output resolution — the size written out. Match it to your platform.

The preview while recording

The preview keeps updating during a recording, and the frames and HUD drawn on it never appear in the file, so you can watch what you are doing without worrying.

Going live instead

Tick Live and no MP4 is written; the output goes straight to your streaming platform. See Live streaming.

Tip

The game-capture preset in The companion tools opens screen capture and Recording what you hear together at preset settings.

Please note

Recording encodes picture and AAC audio at the same time, which asks a fair amount of your PC. If it feels sluggish, lower the FPS or the output resolution.
