Supported formats and titles

A reference list of the file types Raira handles and the game titles whose music loops exactly as it does in game. Handy whenever you wonder "can it read this?"

Audio files

  • Ogg Vorbis, WAV, mp3
  • m4a, aac, alac
  • flac, tta, ape
  • DSD (dsf, dff) and other high-resolution sources
  • Ogg Opus (48 kHz)

Something unusual that fits none of the above may still play through Adding formats with KPI plug-ins.

Video files

avi, mpg, mp4, mkv, wmv, mov, webm and other DirectShow-compatible video. On Windows Vista and later Raira uses EVR for a cleaner picture, and you can switch between video, audio and subtitle streams. See Playing video.

Playlists

m3u, m3u8, pls and xspf can be imported. See Importing playlists from other software.

SASAMI (kbsasami)

Sasami songs play through the kbsasami KPI. Command lists are split by format:

  • .fpy — complete classic FM commands.
  • .fpy2 — delta from .fpy (nest, PCM, EX, …).
  • .mpy — complete classic MIDI commands.
  • .mpw2 — delta from .mpy (64ch, nested loops).
  • .mpsmv — delta from .mpw2 (VST trailer).

How Composer emits those files is SASAMI Composer.

Old-machine sound (bonus)

Zip packs under the data folder near the executable can play as old PC or arcade sound. There is no game screen — audio only. Folders and how to listen are in Bonus: old-machine sound.

Game titles with in-game looping

Ys series

  • Ys VI: The Ark of Napishtim
  • Ys: The Oath in Felghana
  • Ys Origin
  • Ys I & II Complete (Eternal-line Windows ports use this slot)
  • Ys I & II Chronicles
  • Ys: Memories of Celceta (Steam)
  • Ys VIII / Ys IX / Ys X (Steam)

Trails series

  • Trails in the Sky FC / SC / the 3rd, and the Steam remakes the 1st / the 2nd
  • Trails from Zero / Zero Kai / Trails to Azure
  • Trails of Cold Steel I / II / III / IV (Steam)
  • Trails into Reverie (Steam)
  • Trails through Daybreak / Daybreak II (Steam)
  • Trails beyond the Horizon (Steam)

Other Falcom and third-party works

  • Zwei!! (the CD and DVD versions handle audio differently) and Zwei II
  • Xanadu Next, Arcturus, Gurumin
  • Sorcerian Original, Dinosaur Resurrection
  • Brandish 4: The Tower of Sleeping God
  • The Gagharv Trilogy (White Witch, A Tear of Vermillion, Cagesong of the Ocean)
  • Rhapsody of the West Wind, Lunacy of the Moon, Genso Sangokushi 1 / 2
Please note

Brandish 4 and the Gagharv Trilogy play from WAV files copied to your hard disk. Ordinary WAV files that did not come from game data go through DirectShow, so loop and fade-out are not available for them.

Games and built-in engines are tabulated on Support datasheet. Old-machine zips that have been confirmed to play are split across the machine pages from PC-98 through Arcade.

Tip

Exports can be written as WAV, mp3 or FLAC. See Audio export (WAV / mp3 / FLAC).
