Tuner practice

This window puts tuning your instrument and practising to a tempo in one place. Play a single note into the microphone and you get the note name and how many cents out you are, with a click track to practise against.

Using it as a tuner

  1. Choose your microphone under Mic. Press Refresh if it is missing.
  2. Play one note on your instrument.
  3. The note name appears, along with how many cents off it is.
  4. Adjust the instrument until the needle sits in the middle.

The needle covers ±100 cents. Dead centre is in tune.

Reference pitch (A4)

The A4 combo sets the reference pitch, so you can match whatever you are playing with, be that a piano or an orchestra.

Using it as a metronome

  1. Choose the tempo under BPM.
  2. Choose the time signature under Beats.
  3. Choose the device for the click under Click output.
  4. Press Click and it starts.

The first beat of each bar clicks higher, so you can hear where you are in the bar. Tick Mute when you want silence.

Phrase A-B

Press Phrase A-B and Raira builds a practice A-B range around the current playback position on the spot, so "just these four bars, over and over" starts immediately.

This needs the media player, so have a track playing when you press it. See Cues, A-B and practice tempo as well.

Combinations that help practice

  • Slow the music down with Changing tempo and pitch before playing along.
  • Measure the track's tempo in Measuring BPM and use that value for the click.
  • Check the harmony visually in Seeing the music on a piano roll.
  • Keep a record with the practice log in The companion tools.
Please note

This is a hobby tool for single notes. Chords, or a noisy room, will not measure accurately. Use it somewhere quiet, playing one note at a time.
