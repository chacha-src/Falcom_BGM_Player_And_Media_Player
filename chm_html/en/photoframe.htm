The photo frame

This shows the images in a folder you choose, crossfading between them at a regular interval. Just the thing for browsing photos with music playing, or for turning a spare screen into a digital photo frame.

Using it

  1. Use the "..." button beside Folder to choose a folder of images.
  2. Choose how many seconds between images under Interval.
  3. Press Start. Press it again to stop.

Which images it reads

JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP and GIF, up to 512 files from one folder. Images keep their aspect ratio and crossfade smoothly between one another.

The three checkboxes

Shuffle
Randomises the order, so the same folder flows differently each time.
Topmost
Keeps the frame above other windows, which lets you park it somewhere out of the way and leave it running.
BGM
Starts the track selected in the media player, if playback is stopped, so pictures and music begin together.

Ideas

  • Gather screenshots from a game and play its music. Combined with the Falcom BGM screen the atmosphere is rather good.
  • Collect album covers and play the music that goes with them.
  • Include it in a window composition in Recording your screen as a background for a stream.
Tip

The window can be resized freely, and the pictures look considerably better when you give them room.

Please note

If no images are found you are told so — check that the folder contains supported formats. Subfolders are not searched.
