Small things worth knowing

A collection of small things that pay off, which doubles as a checklist for when something is not behaving.

No sound, or odd sound

No sound at all
Check the playback device in Rendering settings. If the app is muted in the Windows volume mixer, Raira warns you at startup.
The audio stutters
Increase the buffer in Rendering settings. Continuous-play crossfade and recording both raise the load.
Turning the volume up is not enough
The main volume, the Equalizer and environmental models master and the extended volume are three separate paths. Check all three.
Muffled, or an effect stuck on
Look for a filter or Kill left engaged on Playing with the DJ pad, and check whether an environmental model is still applied in Equalizer and environmental models.
Thin in mono
Watch the correlation meter in Tools for shaping the sound and adjust the M/S width.

Tracks that cannot be found

  • For game music, check Pointing Raira at your game folders.
  • Missing marks in a playlist can be fixed in bulk with the missing-file review in Working with playlists.
  • Check whether an external drive's letter has changed.

Connections worth remembering

  • Measuring BPM feeds the beat grid, the duration for Continuous-play crossfade and beat nudges on Playing with the DJ pad.
  • Key detection feeds Camelot compatibility for choosing the next track, and the key-to-EQ suggestion.
  • Editing tags feeds online lookup in Showing lyrics and the conditions in Smart playlists and the library tree.
  • Cues, A-B and practice tempo feeds the A-B / cue pack export.
  • Automating a performance with prompts is baked in by "Apply prompt execution" in Audio export (WAV / mp3 / FLAC).

What is remembered per track

BPM, the beat phase offset and the key are stored per track in SongParams.dat and restored the next time you play.

Settings such as volume and EQ are saved against starred tracks when Save per-song is on. Switching it off first saves the current settings to that track, then disables the feature.

When you delete something by mistake

Removed a playlist row? "Undo delete" in the right-click menu brings back one step. It will not go back further, so use it as soon as you notice.

Tip

Whenever you feel lost on screen, right-click where you are. You get a list of what is possible there, and almost every one of those menus includes the operation guide. See The "?" guide on every window.
