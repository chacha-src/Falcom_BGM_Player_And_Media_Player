Playing video

Raira plays avi, mpg, mp4, mkv and more. On Windows Vista and later it uses EVR, which gives a cleaner picture, and you can choose between the video, audio and subtitle streams a file contains.

Playing a file

  1. Drag the video file onto the playlist.
  2. Double-click the row. A video window opens and playback begins.
  3. Nothing visible? You may be missing a codec pack — see the note below.

Switching streams

Where a file carries several audio tracks (say Japanese and English) or subtitle tracks, you can choose which to use. Multiple video streams can be switched too.

Resuming where you left off

For mp3 and DirectShow playback, Raira remembers how far you got — useful for watching something long across several sittings.

Extracting or replacing the audio

The video audio extract / replace tool in The companion tools lets you

  • pull the audio out of a video as a WAV file, or
  • replace a video's audio with a WAV of your own and write out an MP4.

Putting video on a stream

The window composition mode in Recording your screen lets you place the video window and the media player side by side in one output, which can go straight out through Live streaming.

Please note

Some formats, avi among them, may need a codec pack such as K-Lite Codec Pack. There is a guide at ppp.oohara.jp/k-lite.html.

Tip

Video files are skipped automatically during continuous play, so you can keep music and video together in one list and still let the music run on.
