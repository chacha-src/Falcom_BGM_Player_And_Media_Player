Recording what you hear

Open this from the Record button on the media player's bottom row. It captures the loopback of a playback device — everything going to those speakers — so other programs on your PC are recorded along with Raira.

Recording

  1. Press Record on the bottom row.
  2. Playback device — choose whose output you want to capture.
  3. Format — WAV, mp3 or FLAC.
  4. Quality — the bitrate or compression level for mp3 and FLAC.
  5. Save path — use the "..." button to pick a folder and file name.
  6. Press Start recording. Press it again to stop.

Recording your microphone too

Tick Mix microphone and your microphone is blended in.

  • Choose the microphone in the combo below. Press Refresh if it is not listed.
  • Three meters appear: Mic, Sys (system audio) and Mix (the result). One glance tells you which one is too loud.

Reading the meters

A meter pinned to the top means the audio is clipping. Turn the source down, or reduce the microphone mix level. Leaving a little headroom gives you a recording that is much easier to work with afterwards.

Starting a whole session at once

The game-capture preset and live-set recording items in The companion tools open this window together with Recording your screen at preset settings, so you are ready to go without configuring each one.

Tip

mp3 and FLAC go through a temporary WAV encode step, so there is a short wait after you stop. When you are in a hurry, recording straight to WAV is the sure thing.

Please note

To capture only what Raira is playing, Saving to WAV is the better choice — notification sounds from other apps stay out of it.
