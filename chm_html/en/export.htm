Audio export (WAV / mp3 / FLAC)

Open Audio export from the playlist right-click menu. Tabs at the top switch between WAV, mp3 and FLAC. Everything is rendered through the PCM (WAV) path first and encoded afterwards if needed, which is why the same options are available whatever format you pick.

Exporting a single track

  1. Select the track and open Audio export from the right-click menu.
  2. Choose the format with the tabs at the top.
  3. Set the loop count and fade-out.
  4. Choose where to save, and run it.

Options that shape the length

Loop count
For tracks with loop points, how many times to repeat before the end.
Fade-out
Brings the end down gradually, so cutting a loop short never sounds abrupt.
Align leading silence
Evens out the silence at the head of a track: silence longer than your setting is trimmed, and shorter silence is padded out to match. Played back to back, the gaps then feel right.
Sample rate
Leave as source, or choose 44.1, 48, 88.2, 96 or 192 kHz.
Length in seconds
For sources of unknown duration, such as tracks played through Adding formats with KPI plug-ins.

Tags and cover art

  • "Copy tags and cover" carries the original track's information across.
  • Title, artist, album and cover can also be overridden here.
  • To tidy tags before exporting, see Editing tags.

Baking in a performance

Tick "Apply prompt execution" and whatever you wrote in Automating a performance with prompts is rendered into the exported PCM, so tempo and effect moves end up in the file itself.

Joining tracks with a crossfade

Available with several tracks selected. Tracks are joined into one file with equal-power overlaps of the duration you choose. There is also a "Crossfade export" item in the right-click menu.

If you only want seamless listening rather than a file, Continuous-play crossfade is the lighter option.

Layering tracks with Mix

Also a multi-selection option. Choose how many tracks play at once and the volume percentage for each, and they are combined into a single file. Turn Crossfade on as well and finished slots are refilled with the next track over the duration you set, so it never falls silent.

Tip

Capping the export level avoids clipping. See the export limiter and True Peak limiting in Tools for shaping the sound. To even out a whole batch, the loudness normalize batch in The companion tools is the tool for it.

Please note

WAV exports beyond 2 GB switch to RF64, so long exports do not break.
