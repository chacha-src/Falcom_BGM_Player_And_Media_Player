The voice changer

This turns your microphone into a different voice as you speak — male to female, female to male, or effects such as robot and telephone. The result can be used as the microphone input for other applications.

Getting started

  1. Choose your microphone under Input. Press Refresh if it is missing.
  2. Choose where the processed voice goes under Output. If you only want to hear it yourself, speakers or headphones are fine.
  3. Pick a voice from Preset.
  4. Press Start. You can keep adjusting while it runs.

The presets

Basic
Normal (unprocessed), male to female (standard), female to male (standard), young girl, young boy, deep male, robot, radio / phone, chipmunk and custom.
Male to female variants
High-school girl, college girl, higher female, lower female, mature female, grade-school girl and middle-school girl.
Female to male variants
Higher male, lower male, young man, mature male, grade-school boy and middle-school boy.

The individual controls

Pitch
How high the voice sits. Raise it for a female voice.
Form (formant)
Corresponds to the length of the vocal tract. Around 110–120% sounds natural for a female voice. The trick is not to match it to Pitch — setting both the same is what produces that obvious "disguised voice" quality.
Gain
Output level.
Bright
The presence of the voice.
Breath
How much breath is in it.
Quality
Trades latency against fidelity. Low latency suits conversation; high quality suits recording.
FX
None, robot, or radio / phone.

Hearing yourself

Tick Local monitor and the processed voice comes back to you, which makes adjusting far easier.

Using it in other applications

For a chat program such as Discord, choose a virtual audio cable as the output here, then set that same device as the microphone input in the chat program.

Putting it on a recording or stream

Turn on Apply VC in Recording your screen or in the microphone mix of Saving to WAV and the same preset is applied to the microphone there, so you do not have to configure it twice.

Tip

The engine uses LPC to separate the vocal tract from the rest, then always applies the pitch conversion to the glottal residual. That is why even quiet speech is converted rather than slipping through unchanged.

Please note

If the output device you chose is in use by Sending sound to a second device, you get an output-conflict message. Move one of them to another device.
