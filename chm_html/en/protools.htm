Tools for shaping the sound

Playback details carries a set of tools for adjusting the stereo image and how prominent the vocal is. You do not need the theory — press a preset and let your ears decide whether it got better.

Playback details (sound tools)

Cancelling or emphasising the vocal

This weakens or strengthens whatever sits in the centre of the mix, which is usually the vocal.

0 (leaning to cancel)
Reduces the centre content. Usable as a karaoke track, though it never disappears entirely.
100 (neutral)
The sound as it is.
200 (emphasise)
Strengthens the centre. Useful for picking out a voice or transcribing lyrics.

The slider takes effect while you drag it. Right-click for instant presets: vocal cancel (0), neutral (100) and emphasise Mid (200).

M/S width

  • 100% leaves things as they are.
  • Lower pulls the sound towards the centre for a tighter image.
  • Higher spreads it out for a more open feel.
  • 0 collapses everything to the centre.

The correlation-to-M/S button reads the tendency of the correlation meter and applies a width that suits it. By hand there are presets for narrow (40%), wide (160%), mono and reset.

The phase correlation meter

This shows how similar the channels are, from -1 to +1. A strong negative reading suggests the sound may thin out in mono. The same value appears in Jacket art and the banner display.

Export limiter and True Peak

  • Export limit caps the level on export so nothing clips.
  • Ceiling sets how far up that cap sits, for instance -1.0 dB.
  • True Peak limit looks at peaks hidden between samples. Use it when you want to avoid clipping after converting to mp3 or similar.

Levelling a whole batch

To even out many tracks at once, the loudness normalize batch in The companion tools exports your selection towards a target loudness, with the limiter alongside.

Tip

Vocal cancel is also a one-touch control on Playing with the DJ pad, so you can pull the voice out and drop it back mid-track.

Please note

Cancelling the vocal reduces all the centre content, which means bass and kick thin out with it. How well it works varies enormously between tracks, so start from 100 and move in small steps.
