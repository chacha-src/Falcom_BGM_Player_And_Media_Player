Tuning piano roll detection

This window controls how sensitive the piano roll is. There are a lot of sliders, but the reliable approach is to move them a little at a time and judge with your eyes and ears. If it all goes wrong, "Reset to defaults" starts you over.

Opening it

Right-click the piano roll display and choose the detection tuning dialog.

How to go about it

  1. Open it while the track you care about is playing — adjusting against a moving display is far clearer.
  2. Move one slider at a time. Change several at once and you lose track of which one did what.
  3. Picking up too much? Raise the threshold. Picking up too little? Lower it.
  4. If you paint yourself into a corner, press "Reset to defaults" and begin again.

Common symptoms

ProblemWhat to try
Notes so dense you cannot read themRaise the threshold; narrow the range to 88 keys
Nothing appears in quiet passagesLower the threshold, or raise the volume first
Percussion registers as notesReduce sensitivity in the low bands
Chord names keep flickeringMove settings towards a steadier judgement
High notes never appearSwitch the range to 108 keys

Worth trying afterwards

  • Capture MIDI or MusicXML in Seeing the music on a piano roll.
  • Open the Waveform and spectrum analyzer alongside and compare the frequency peaks with the notes detected.
  • Confirm the tempo in Measuring BPM so beats and notes line up.
Tip

This window has a "?" button too, with a diagram of what each slider affects. See The "?" guide on every window.

Please note

The best values depend heavily on how the music is put together. There is no universal setting, so it is practical to settle on something that suits the genre you listen to most.
