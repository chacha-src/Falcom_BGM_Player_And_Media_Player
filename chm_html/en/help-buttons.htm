The "?" guide on every window

Nearly every window carries a small ? button. Press it and you get an operation guide drawn for that screen. It is often clearer than words alone, which makes it the best first move whenever you wonder what a control does.

Book (offline help) and ? buttons on the caption bar

Where to find it

  • The ? button, usually near the top right (guide for that window only).
  • The 本 (book) button just to the left of ? — opens this full offline help (CHM).
  • The F1 key — same as the book button; opens offline help.
  • The Operation guide item in the right-click context menu (same as ?).

Difference between ? and the book button

?
A short illustrated sheet for the window you are looking at. Handy when you just want to find a control.
Book / F1
The full offline help for every feature. Use the contents, keyword search, and previous/next links. Japanese opens by default; if Windows is set to English, the English pages open first. At the top of the contents you can switch between Japanese and English.

What the diagrams cover

Besides a minimap of the window, the guides illustrate the parts that are hardest to describe in words.

  • Seek layers — how the waveform, beat grid, LRC markers and the loop and A-B ranges stack up (Reading the seek bar)
  • Camelot compatibility — how neighbouring keys and candidates work
  • Set chapters — arranging Warmup, Peak and Cooldown
  • Remote / OBS — how browser control and the overlay relate (Controlling Raira from a phone or PC)
Tip

When you cannot place where a newly added feature fits, opening the operation guide is the quickest way in. Reading the matching page here afterwards fills in the rest.
