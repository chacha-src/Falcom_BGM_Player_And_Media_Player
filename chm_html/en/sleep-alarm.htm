Sleep timer and alarm

The sleep timer stops playback after a number of minutes. The alarm starts playback at a time you choose. Between them, both ends of the day are covered.

The sleep timer

  1. Open the sleep timer from the media player.
  2. Set how many minutes until it stops. A custom value lets you pick any number.
  3. A countdown is displayed so you can see how long is left.

Ideal for music that carries you off to sleep. A playlist of quiet tracks makes it nicer still — a rule in Smart playlists and the library tree for "quiet tracks" is one good way to build one.

The alarm

  1. Open the alarm window.
  2. Choose Hour and Min.
  3. Tick Enable.

Playback begins at the time you set — a pleasant way to wake up to something you actually like.

Things to bear in mind

  • The alarm will not sound if the PC has gone to sleep or hibernated, so set your power options to stay awake.
  • Set the volume too low and you will not hear it. Check the night before that it is loud enough.
  • The alarm plays the currently selected track, so select what you want to hear in advance.

Starting gently

If waking to a sudden noise is not for you, combine Continuous-play crossfade with a playlist that opens quietly and builds from there.

Tip

The alarm can also be opened from the Tools menu in The companion tools. The sleep timer stays where it has always been, on the media player.
