A tour of the media player screen

The media player screen reads from top to bottom: track information and jacket art, then the seek bar, then the transport controls, then the playlist, and finally a row of buttons along the bottom. Keep that map in mind and you will never feel lost.

Main screen of media player Raira

The layout

+--------------------------------------------------+
| jacket   title / artist      banner + meters     |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| seek bar (waveform, beat grid, loop, A-B)        |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| play stop prev next  volume  random cont. loops  |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| playlist (title, time, tags, star rating)        |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| Record Capture DJ BPM Mirror Remote Maze Race    |
+--------------------------------------------------+

Up top — the face of the track

Jacket art, the track title and a ring showing remaining time. To their right sit a banner that moves with the music and a left-right balance meter. See Jacket art and the banner display.

The middle — seek bar and transport

The seek bar is the most carefully built part of the program. Waveform, beat grid, lyric timings, loop range and A-B range are all drawn on top of one another. Reading the seek bar takes it layer by layer.

Play, stop, previous and next, volume, random play and continuous play are covered in Playing, stopping and volume.

Down below — the playlist and the toolbox

The playlist holds your tracks, but it is also a way in: right-click a row and you get everything you can do to that track, from tag editing to export. See Working with playlists.

Each button on the bottom row opens its own window.

ButtonOpens
RecordRecording what you hear
CaptureRecording your screen
DJPlaying with the DJ pad
BPMMeasuring BPM
MirrorSending sound to a second device
RemoteControlling Raira from a phone or PC
MazeBonus: the Soft3D maze
RaceBonus: the Soft3D aerial race

Other windows you can open

  • Extra opens Playback details — gapless, loops, cues and tags in one place.
  • Ana opens the Waveform and spectrum analyzer.
  • Roll opens Seeing the music on a piano roll.
  • Lyrics shows Showing lyrics. Right-click the arrow for the always-on-top lyrics window.
  • Prompt opens Automating a performance with prompts.
  • ? shows a diagram of the current screen. See The "?" guide on every window.
Tip

Try right-clicking anywhere on the screen. You get a menu suited to that spot, and almost every one of them includes "Acrylic mode" and "Operation guide".

Please note

Moving the main window drags the open companion windows along with it. To place one independently, turn off follow-lock in its right-click menu.
