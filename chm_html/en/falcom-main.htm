The Falcom BGM screen

The Falcom BGM screen exists to play music from the Ys and Trails series (and more) exactly as it played in the game — including that satisfying jump back past the intro at the end of a track.

From launch to listening

  1. Choose a game title. Supported titles are listed in Supported formats and titles.
  2. The music list for that game appears. Click a track name to play it.
  3. Empty list, or silence? Revisit Pointing Raira at your game folders.

How the looping works

Game music has a defined "when you reach here, go back to there" point. Raira carries the loop information for each supported title, so at the end of a track it skips the intro and returns to the natural point. You can also say how many times to loop before moving on.

Want to set the loop yourself? Playback details lets you edit the loop in and out points and the fade at the loop boundary.

Writing tracks out to WAV

Selected tracks can be converted and saved as WAV files. Because you can set loop count and fade-out, it is easy to produce something pleasant to listen to — say, two loops then a gentle fade. See Saving to WAV for the steps.

If you want mp3 or FLAC instead, use Audio export (WAV / mp3 / FLAC) on the media player screen.

What you can open from here

  • Equalizer and environmental models — change the character of the sound
  • Rendering settings — playback device and language
  • Pointing Raira at your game folders — tell Raira where to look
  • The "?" guide on every window — a diagram of this screen
Please note

For Brandish 4 and the Gagharv Trilogy you must copy the WAV files to your hard disk first. The folder is named WAVE, WAVEDV or WAVEDVD depending on the game.

Tip

Ordinary WAV files that did not come from game data are handled through DirectShow, so loop and fade-out are unavailable for them. Those are more comfortable in the media player screen.
