Controlling Raira from a phone or PC

Turn the local remote on and any phone or PC on the same Wi-Fi or LAN can control Raira from a browser. Up to six clients at once. Nothing is exposed to the public internet.

Local remote settings

Connecting

  1. Press the Remote button on the media player's bottom row.
  2. Tick HTTP on Wi-Fi/LAN.
  3. An address of the form http://(LAN IP):port/ appears.
  4. Open that address in your phone's browser. From the same PC, 127.0.0.1 works too.
Please note

The first time you turn this on, Windows Firewall may ask for permission. Without it, other devices cannot connect.

The browser tabs

Transport
Play, stop, previous, next, volume — the essentials.
EQ
Reach Equalizer and environmental models from across the room.
Playlist
Browse the list and pick something to play.
Lyrics
Read Showing lyrics on your phone screen.
DJ scratch
Drag a record with your finger. A slice of Playing with the DJ pad, brought along with you.
Piano
Play notes from an on-screen keyboard, which pairs with Playing instruments with the VST host.
Analyzer
View the Waveform and spectrum analyzer in the browser.

Outputs useful for streaming

OBS overlay
Open http://.../overlay for simple HTML with a transparent background showing the track and state. Drop it into an OBS browser source to put the title on your stream. See Live streaming.
Shared queue
.../api/queue-add?i=row adds that row to the up-next queue, which lets people in the room choose what plays next. It is meant for local use and has no authentication.
AAC listen stream
Turn on the AAC listen stream and the audio can be heard in a browser. Quality options are 128, 192 and low-latency 96 kbps.

Changing the port

If another program already uses the port, change the number here — then use the new number in the address on your phone.

Tip

This is at its most useful mid-stream, or when music is playing and you are nowhere near the keyboard. Keeping it open alongside Live streaming means you can change tracks with your hands full.

Please note

This feature is not published to the internet. It assumes a network you trust and therefore has no authentication, so do not use it somewhere strangers can connect, such as public Wi-Fi.
