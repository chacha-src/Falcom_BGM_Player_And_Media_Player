The companion tools

These handy extras open from the Tools right-click menu on the media player, and from the right-click menu of individual windows. There are a lot of them, so this page is a reference of what each one does. Try whatever catches your eye.

Lyrics and notation

Lyrics window
Always-on-top lyrics that remember opacity, line count, font and position. LRC timing shifts of ±10, ±50 and ±100 ms can be saved to the file. See Showing lyrics.
Piano-roll extras
Experimental MIDI / MusicXML capture during playback, a chord progression panel, and turning your PC's own audio into a score. See Seeing the music on a piano roll.
Spectrogram jacket
Saves the Waveform and spectrum analyzer view as an image.

Shaping and exporting audio

A-B / cue pack export
Writes your A-B and cue ranges out as numbered files. Pairs with Cues, A-B and practice tempo.
Loudness normalize batch
Exports the selected tracks towards a target loudness, with the limiter alongside.
Vocal cancel / emphasise
Weakens or strengthens the centre content, driven from Tools for shaping the sound.
M/S presets
One-click narrow, wide or mono from the correlation tools.
Key to EQ suggestion
Proposes an Equalizer and environmental models preset from the detected key.
Video audio extract / replace
Pulls a WAV out of a video, or replaces a video's audio with your own WAV and writes an MP4. See Playing video.

Tags and information

MusicBrainz auto-tag
Looks up candidates by title and artist and applies the tags. No audio fingerprinting. See Editing tags.
nowplaying.txt
Writes the current track name to a file in the same location as the executable, ready for a stream overlay. See Live streaming.

Performing and DJing

BPM measure
See Measuring BPM.
Key detect and Camelot compatibility
Stores the analysed key per track and lists tracks in neighbouring (compatible) keys from the current playlist as candidates for what to play next.
DJ pad
See Playing with the DJ pad.
MIDI keyboard control
See Controlling Raira from a MIDI keyboard.
Transition presets
Applies combinations of EQ sweep, filter and crossfade duration aimed at the next track. Timeline automation itself lives in Laying commands out on the roll.

Recording and sharing

Live-set recording
Opens Recording your screen and Recording what you hear together.
Game-capture preset
Much the same, opened with settings suited to games. Local recording, not internet streaming.
Output mirror
See Sending sound to a second device.
Local remote
See Controlling Raira from a phone or PC.
Screensaver-style visualizer
A fullscreen display that moves with the music. ESC exits.

Fitting around your day

Alarm
Starts playback at a set time. See Sleep timer and alarm.
Focus mode
Reduces the clutter on screen so you can concentrate.
Dangerous-action confirmation
Asks before anything destructive.
Layouts 1–3
Remembers up to three window arrangements to recall.
Weekly summary and practice log
Look back at how much you have listened or practised. Read with Looking back at play history.
Undo list delete
"Undo delete" in the right-click menu brings back the last deletion only. It is not a full undo history.
Tip

Several of these also open as windows of their own: Tuner practice, The sound meter, The analog digitizer, The voice changer, The photo frame and The CD player.
