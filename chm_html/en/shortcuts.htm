Shortcuts and the command palette

The things you do most can be reached from the keyboard or the taskbar directly. There is no need to learn them all at once, and when you cannot remember where a feature lives, typing its name into the command palette is the fastest route.

The command palette

A small window where you type the name of a feature to find it. Instead of walking through menus, type part of a name and go straight there — ideal when you have forgotten where something is.

Controlling Raira from the taskbar

Jump list
Right-click the taskbar icon. Recently played tracks, replay, stop, previous and next, Equalizer and environmental models, the jacket, Rendering settings and folders are all reachable.
Thumbnail toolbar
Hover the taskbar button and the preview carries small buttons for replay, pause, stop and next.

Controlling it from further away

When you are away from the machine, Controlling Raira from a phone or PC is the answer. With MIDI hardware to hand, Controlling Raira from a MIDI keyboard assigns controls to real keys and knobs.

Tip

To leave a fullscreen display such as the Soft3D maze or the visualizer, press ESC. When in doubt, try ESC first.
