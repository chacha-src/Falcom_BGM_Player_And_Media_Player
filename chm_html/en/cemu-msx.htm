CEmu: MSX

MSX zips that have been confirmed to play, including KSS. They live in data\msx.

CPU

The CPU is the Z80.

Sound chips

ColumnPartWhat it is
PSGAY-3-8910The MSX PSG.
OPLLYM2413FM-PAC / MSX-MUSIC.

Confirmed MSX packs are assigned both PSG and OPLL. KSS in Notes means the song data is KSS. A zip that is not listed still will not play.
