Playback details

Open this from the Extra button on the media player or from the playlist right-click menu. The finer playback settings live here alongside the track's own loop, cues and tags, and most options take effect immediately.

Playback details

Playback and level

Gapless
Tightens the silence between tracks during continuous play. Just what live albums and continuous mixes need.
ReplayGain
Off, Track or Album. Evens out level differences between tracks or albums automatically, with a target loudness you can set.
Stereo width
How wide the image is. 100 is normal, lower is narrower, higher is wider, and 0 collapses to the centre.
Mono compatibility check
Suppresses the side content so you can check nothing disappears when heard in mono.
Left-right balance
Shows whether left and right are in step. The same meter sits beside the media player banner.
Vocal Mid
0 leans towards cancelling, 100 is neutral, 200 emphasises. Right-click for presets. See Tools for shaping the sound.
Export limiter
Caps the level on export to prevent clipping. You can set a ceiling and also stop the file breaking up after conversion.

Loop and cues

  • Click the waveform to set the in point and Shift+click for the out point. Green is in, red is out.
  • Enter loop in, loop out and the boundary fade in milliseconds, then Apply. It takes effect from the next loop.
  • The "set from current position" buttons use wherever playback has reached.
  • Cues can be added, deleted and jumped to at the current position, and they stay with the track. See Cues, A-B and practice tempo.

Tags

View title, artist, album, year, track, genre and comment, and write them straight to the file. The comment field can also carry LOOPSTART, LOOPEND and LOOPLENGTH, so loop points can live inside the file itself.

Writing is supported for MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A and Ogg Vorbis. For fixing many tracks at once, Editing tags is the better tool.

Closing the window

Apply commits the loop and similar values. OK or Close shuts the window, and anything already applied stays applied.

Tip

The "?" button on this window draws out where everything sits. See The "?" guide on every window.

Please note

Encrypted FLAC and writing tags to Opus are not supported.
