Sending sound to a second device

Output mirroring sends what is playing to a second playback device at the same time. Use it when you want sound from both the room speakers and your headphones, or when you want to check the mix on other equipment.

Mirror display

Setting it up

  1. Press the Mirror button on the media player's bottom row.
  2. Choose the destination under Device.
  3. Tick Mirror on.
  4. Use the Vol slider to set the mirror's level on its own.

Separate volume, identical sound

The only thing you can set independently on the mirror is volume. The equalizer and effects are the same as on the main output, so having different EQ on each side is not possible.

Mirror CUE

There is a Mirror CUE toggle with its own independent gain — the DJ monitor idea, letting you check on one side while the other keeps playing.

Combinations worth trying

  • Speakers as the main output, headphones as the mirror, so you can listen closely yourself.
  • An audio interface as the main output and the built-in speakers as the mirror, to confirm the connection is alive.
  • Pair it with Recording your screen or Recording what you hear to separate the recording path from the monitoring path.
Tip

If the two outputs sound smeared together, the cause is the difference in latency between the devices. Adjusting the buffer in Rendering settings brings them closer. Alternatively, accept one side as a monitor rather than trying to hear both at once.

Please note

If the output you chose is already in use by The voice changer, Raira tells you about the conflict. Move one of them to a different device.
