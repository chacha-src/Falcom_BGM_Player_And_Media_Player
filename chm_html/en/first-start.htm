Your first launch

The first time you run Raira it asks you a few things. None of the answers are permanent, so pick whatever feels right. This page covers the few minutes of setup that make everything afterwards more comfortable.

Example of the main screen after launch

1. Choose your starting screen

A small dialog appears asking whether to start in the Falcom BGM screen or the media player screen.

  • Want game music with proper loops? Choose the Falcom BGM screen.
  • Want playlists of your own music and video? Choose the media player screen.

Clear the Ask again next time checkbox and Raira will simply start in your chosen screen from then on. Changed your mind? The two screen modes shows how to switch at any time.

2. Pick your language

The language setting lives in Rendering settings. Fourteen languages are available: Japanese, English, Français, Italiano, Español, 한국어, 中文, العربية, Русский, Deutsch, Português, Nederlands, Polski and Türkçe.

3. Check where the sound goes

When there is no sound at all, the playback device is usually the culprit. Pick your speakers or headphones again in Rendering settings. If the app itself is muted in the Windows volume mixer, Raira warns you at startup.

4. For game music, point Raira at your games

Falcom music is read straight out of the folder where the game is installed. The standard install paths are filled in by default, so it often just works. If a game is not found, set its folder in Pointing Raira at your game folders.

5. For your own music, just drag and drop

  1. Open the media player screen.
  2. Drag files or whole folders from Explorer onto the playlist.
  3. Double-click a track to start playing.
Tip

Associating your usual file types with Raira means you can play anything by double-clicking it in Explorer. Rendering settings covers audio, video and playlist associations in one place.

Please note

If avi files will not play, you probably need a codec pack such as K-Lite Codec Pack. See Playing video for details.
