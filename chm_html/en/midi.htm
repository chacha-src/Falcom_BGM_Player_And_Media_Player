Controlling Raira from a MIDI keyboard

Connect a MIDI keyboard or controller and you can drive Raira with notes and CC messages from knobs and faders. Take your hands off the mouse and treat it like an instrument.

What you can do

  • Press keys to play, stop or skip to the next track.
  • Use a knob or fader for volume.
  • Drive tempo and the bands of Equalizer and environmental models continuously from a knob.
  • Bring the controls of Playing with the DJ pad onto your own hardware.

Assigning with MIDI learn

  1. Choose the function you want to assign — tempo, for instance.
  2. Put MIDI learn into its waiting state.
  3. Move the knob on your controller. Its CC is captured.
  4. From then on, that knob drives the tempo.

Assignments are saved, so the same controller works the same way the next time you start up.

When you want to make sound

To play notes into an instrument, use Playing instruments with the VST host. Up to three MIDI inputs are accepted and fed to the parts in real time. For playlist .mid through a VST see Playing .mid through a VST; for channel state see the MIDI monitor.

How this relates to the piano roll

Seeing the music on a piano roll picks up the audio and writes it out as MIDI or MusicXML. That is transcribing the music that is playing, not recording what you perform.

Tip

No MIDI hardware? The piano tab in Controlling Raira from a phone or PC turns a phone screen into a keyboard.

Please note

If a device is not recognised after you plug it in, restart the program. Another application holding the device open will also stop it being seen.
