Reading the seek bar

The seek bar is far more than a bar you drag. Several layers of information are drawn on top of each other, and once you can read them you can see at a glance where everything in a track is.

The layers

+--------------------------------------------------+
|  waveform overview (furthest back)               |
|  | | | |   beat grid (Alt+drag shifts phase)     |
|  : : :     LRC markers in cyan (click to seek)   |
|  ####pink = loop####  blue A---B   play position |
+--------------------------------------------------+
Waveform overview
The shape of the track's loudness. It fills in live as you play; WAV files are replaced by an overview of the whole file. Toggle it from the right-click menu.
Beat grid
Thin vertical lines marking the beats, based on the tempo measured in Measuring BPM. If the lines sit slightly off the real beat, hold Alt and drag on the seek bar to shift the phase. That offset is remembered per track alongside BPM and key.
LRC markers
Cyan lines showing the timings in your lyrics file. Click one to jump to that line, which also makes them handy candidates for A-B points. Hovering pops up a magnifier of that area.
Loop range (pink)
The game loop range, shown as a pink band with its own thumbs. A lock checkbox freezes the thumbs so you cannot nudge them by accident (locked by default).
A-B range (blue)
Your own repeat range. Different colour, different thumbs, so it never gets confused with the loop.
Spectrum ribbon
A thin frequency display along the seek bar, so you can follow the shape of the track with your eyes.

What the mouse does

  • Click seeks to that position.
  • Hover pops up a tip with the time at that point.
  • Drag moves the loop or A-B thumbs.
  • Alt+drag shifts the beat-grid phase.
  • Right-click gives you fine adjustment of seek, loop and A-B, setting the A and B points, adding cues and toggling the waveform.

How many times A-B has looped

While an A-B range repeats, the number after L counts the passes. Useful when you are drilling a phrase until it sticks.

Tip

If the waveform is too small to read, open the Waveform and spectrum analyzer, which gives you a large scrolling waveform and the frequency response side by side.

Please note

Beat grid and key are stored per track in SongParams.dat and restored next time you play. If an adjustment seems to have vanished, check the per-song (star) state in Viewing and fixing file info.
